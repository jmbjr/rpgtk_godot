Studio resources, processes and values

Resources

One director role is recorded. Art/development roles, willingness and sprint availability await confirmation. Tools are recorded separately with implementation evidence.

Processes

Brief → create → submit → review → select exact revisions → verify in Play. Local submission is proposed in #146; review/build services remain planned. Manual records can retain decisions meanwhile.

Values

People own creative choices and art. AI helps coordinate, implement and check. Respect willingness; disclose unknowns; keep private originals local; stay practical for five people.
