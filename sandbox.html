What this experiment proves

Four-way controls, walking animation, solid-tile collisions, and a stateful inn conversation with first/repeat dialogue, a clue counter and a source-backed cooldown rule.

What it does not prove: Toolkit collision-vector semantics, imported animation parity, original RPGCode execution, save loading, COM plugin compatibility or exact BSTW interaction range.

The inn dialogue uses newly written lines and an explicit deterministic adapter modeled on the observed mwininn.prg branch and clue updates. The inn stays locked. Reset clears clues and visits.
