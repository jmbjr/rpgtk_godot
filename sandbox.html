What this experiment proves

Four-way controls, visible walking frames, normalized diagonal motion, solid-tile collisions, wall sliding and phone-friendly touch input.

What it does not prove: Toolkit collision-vector semantics, imported animation parity, actual RPGCode execution, door dialogue, save loading or COM plugin compatibility.
